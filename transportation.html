Practical travel planning

Make your first visit feel manageable

Rail transit

BTS Skytrain and MRT routes can help avoid road traffic on many journeys. Check current maps, fares and operating hours.

Boats and local connections

River boats can be useful for some riverside attractions. Verify the pier, route and service before leaving.

Road transportation

For taxis and ride-hailing, confirm pickup details and the fare method. Allow extra time when roads are busy.
