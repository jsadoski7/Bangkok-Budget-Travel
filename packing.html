Practical travel planning

Make your first visit feel manageable

Documents and essentials

Bring your passport, travel documents, payment options, medications and digital backups stored securely.

Clothing and weather

Choose lightweight clothing, comfortable walking shoes and a compact rain layer. Check forecasts close to departure.

Everyday convenience

Consider a suitable power adapter, reusable water bottle, sun protection and a small day bag. Check airline baggage rules.
