# A/B Test Plan: Bangkok on a Budget

## 1. Test Description

This experiment will test whether changing the homepage headline affects an AI visitor's interest in exploring the Bangkok on a Budget website.

- **Version A:** The existing homepage headline, "Bangkok on a Budget."
- **Version B:** "Plan Your First Bangkok Trip Without Breaking the Bank."
- **Element being tested:** Homepage headline.
- **Everything else:** The same between both versions.

## 2. Hypothesis

I believe Version B will perform better than Version A because it more clearly communicates the website's purpose and emphasizes affordable travel for first-time visitors to Bangkok. Since the target audience is college students planning their first trip to Bangkok on a budget, I expect the more descriptive headline will make visitors more interested in exploring the website's itinerary page.

## 3. AI Visitor and Testing Method

The experiment will use AI visitors representing college students who are planning their first trip to Bangkok and are interested in traveling on a budget.

Each trial will use a fresh AI conversation with the same AI model and settings. Each visitor will receive the same prompt, with only the assigned website version changing.

Visitors will be randomly assigned to Version A or Version B before testing begins.

## 4. Primary Outcome

The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor says they would explore the itinerary page after reviewing the homepage.

The result will be recorded as either YES or NO.

The percentage of YES responses will be calculated for each version.

## 5. Sample Size and Testing Period

The experiment will include at least 100 total trials:

- 50 trials for Version A.
- 50 trials for Version B.

The experiment will stop after all 100 trials are completed or on the predetermined stop date, whichever comes first. If the minimum sample size has not been reached by the stop date, the limitation will be documented.

## 6. Comparison and Decision Criteria

The percentage of YES responses for Version A will be compared with the percentage for Version B.

Version B will be considered more promising if it has a higher percentage of YES responses. A statistical test will also be used to determine whether the observed difference provides evidence of a meaningful difference.

The results will be reported honestly, including if the experiment does not show a clear difference.
