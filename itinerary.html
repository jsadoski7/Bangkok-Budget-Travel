Practical travel planning

Make your first visit feel manageable

Day 1: Get oriented

Check in, explore a nearby neighborhood and try a local market or food court. Keep your first day flexible after travel.

Day 2: Riverside highlights

Explore cultural attractions near the river, checking opening hours, entry rules and respectful dress expectations in advance.

Day 3: City exploration

Try a transit-connected neighborhood, visit a park or museum, and leave time to revisit a favorite place.
